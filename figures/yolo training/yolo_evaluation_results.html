SYSTEM TESTING

This section presents the phase of system testing and evaluation of the Face Detection System using YOLOv8n. The researchers evaluated the developed system to determine its performance and learning behavior throughout the training process. The YOLOv8n architecture was trained and tested over 100 epochs to generate performance metrics, including mAP50, Recall, Precision, and F1-score. Table 1 summarizes the performance of the YOLOv8n architecture across different epoch settings.
